01 / ABOUT

A little about me

I am Kathan Desai, a PhD student in astrophysics at the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ), where I have joined a research project involving supernova simulations.

My academic path combines electrical engineering and physics. I completed a bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering at Dr. S. & S. S. Gandhy Government Engineering College in Gujarat, India, followed by an MS in Physics at San Diego State University in the United States.

During my master's research, I studied nucleon–nucleon short-range correlations in neutron-star matter, including how modifications to the nucleon momentum distribution affect the neutron-star equation of state. I am interested in using theoretical and computational physics to understand extreme astrophysical environments.

About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ). My research focuses on computational astrophysics and the physical processes involved in stellar explosions.

Before beginning my doctoral research, I completed an MS in Physics at San Diego State University, where I studied nucleon–nucleon short-range correlations in dense nuclear matter and their relevance to neutron-star physics.

Research Interests

Supernova Simulations

Investigating the dynamics of stellar explosions through numerical simulations.

Hydrodynamic Instabilities

Studying fluid instabilities, mixing, and nonlinear dynamics in astrophysical environments.

Neutron-Star Physics

Exploring dense nuclear matter and the physics of compact astrophysical objects.
